02 · ABOUT

Meet Waqas

WAQAS ALBLOUSHI USGTF PRO
GOLF PROFESSIONAL · DOHA

Coaching built around clarity.

Waqas Albloushi is a professional golf coach, competitive player and mentor with more than 14 years in the game. He started playing golf in 2007 at the age of 17 and has built his experience through playing, coaching, competition and continuous development.

Waqas continues to represent Qatar and compete in tournaments, keeping him connected to the competitive side of the game. His coaching focuses on building a strong foundation, improving technique and consistency, developing course strategy and building the confidence needed to perform on the course.

Golf is for everyone: men and women, beginners and experienced players, young or old. Coaching is tailored to each individual, regardless of their level or background.
